⚡ Messi Fast Facts

Quick bite-sized details every fan should know!

Born & Raised

June 24, 1987 in Rosario, Argentina. He began playing football at age 4!

Nickname: "La Pulga"

Means "The Flea" in Spanish because he is short, quick, and hard for defenders to catch!

Point to the Sky

Whenever he scores, he points two fingers to the sky to dedicate his goals to his late grandmother, Celia.

Overcoming Obstacles

As a child, he was diagnosed with Growth Hormone Deficiency. FC Barcelona agreed to pay for his medical treatment.

The Napkin Contract

His first contract with Barcelona was written on a paper napkin at a tennis club restaurant!

Clubs Played For

Grandoli, Newell's Old Boys, FC Barcelona (2004–2021), PSG (2021–2023), Inter Miami (2023–Present).
