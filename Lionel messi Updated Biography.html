1987 - 2000
Rosario, Argentina ➔ Barcelona

Rosario Childhood & The Napkin Contract

Lionel Messi was born on June 24, 1987. Diagnosed with growth hormone deficiency as a child, FC Barcelona agreed to pay for his treatment and signed his first agreement on a famous paper napkin in December 2000!

Key Milestone: Joined La Masia youth academy at age 13.

2004 - 2005
FC Barcelona #30 Debut

First Senior Goal vs Albacete

On May 1, 2005, a 17-year-old Messi scored his first official senior goal for FC Barcelona against Albacete after a delicate chip assist from Ronaldinho, who carried young Messi on his back to celebrate!

Key Milestone: Youngest goalscorer in Barcelona history at the time.
Click to Enlarge
2009

Historic Sextuple & 1st Ballon d'Or

Under Pep Guardiola, Barcelona became the first team in football history to win all 6 trophies in a single calendar year (La Liga, Copa del Rey, UCL, Spanish Super Cup, UEFA Super Cup, FIFA Club World Cup).

Key Milestone: Scored iconic header in Rome UCL Final vs Man United.
Click to Enlarge
2012

91 Goals in a Calendar Year

Messi accomplished the greatest individual scoring feat in football history, scoring an astonishing 91 goals in 69 official matches for Barcelona and Argentina, breaking Gerd Müller's 1972 record (85 goals).

Key Milestone: Won 4th consecutive Ballon d'Or award.

2022 WORLD CUP

FIFA World Cup Champion 🏆

The ultimate masterpiece! Messi scored 2 goals in the epic final against France (3-3 aet, 4-2 pens) and received the FIFA Golden Ball trophy as the tournament's best player.

Qatar 2022 Journey

2022 FIFA World Cup • Road to Glory 🏆

Follow Messi's step-by-step match-by-match journey from group stage shock to World Cup immortality.

MATCH 1 • GROUP C
LUSAIL STADIUM
🇦🇷 Arg 1 - 2 KSA 🇸🇦

Shock Defeat & Messi's Rally Call

Messi opened the scoring with a 10th-minute penalty, but 2 quick goals from Saudi Arabia broke Argentina's 36-game unbeaten streak. After the match, Messi famously rallied fans: "Don't lose faith, we won't leave you stranded."

Messi Goal: 1 Penalty (10')
MATCH 2 • GROUP C
LUSAIL STADIUM
🇦🇷 Arg 2 - 0 Mex 🇲🇽

The Clutch Long-Range Strike

Facing elimination, Messi produced sheer magic in the 64th minute, drilling a pinpoint 25-yard low strike past Ochoa before assisting Enzo Fernández's late curler to revive Argentina's World Cup dream.

Messi: 1 Goal (64'), 1 Assist
MATCH 3 • GROUP C
STADIUM 974
🇦🇷 Arg 2 - 0 Pol 🇵🇱

Securing Top Spot in Group C

Despite Szczęsny saving Messi's first-half penalty, Argentina put on a masterclass performance. Alexis Mac Allister and Julián Álvarez scored second-half goals to secure 1st place in Group C.

Result: Qualified 1st in Group C
ROUND OF 16
AHMAD BIN ALI STADIUM
🇦🇷 Arg 2 - 1 Aus 🇦🇺

Messi's 1,000th Career Match Magic

Playing his milestone 1,000th official senior career match, Messi opened the scoring in the 35th minute with his first-ever knockout World Cup goal. Lisandro Martínez & Emi Martínez made clutch late saves!

Messi Goal: 35th Min Curler
QUARTER-FINAL
LUSAIL STADIUM
🇦🇷 Arg 2 - 2 Ned 🇳🇱
(Argentina won 4 - 3 on pens)

The No-Look Assist & Epic Shootout

Messi delivered an impossible no-look reverse pass for Nahuel Molina's goal and scored a penalty. After a dramatic late 2-2 equalizer by Weghorst, Emi Martínez saved 2 shootout penalties!

Messi: 1 Goal, 1 Impossible Assist
SEMI-FINAL
LUSAIL STADIUM
🇦🇷 Arg 3 - 0 Cro 🇭🇷

Masterclass & Dribble vs Gvardiol

Messi blasted a 34th-minute penalty top corner before providing the tournament's greatest assist—dribbling 40 yards and turning defender Joško Gvardiol inside-out to set up Julián Álvarez.

Messi: 1 Goal, 1 Assist of the Tournament
GRAND FINAL 🏆
LUSAIL STADIUM • DEC 18, 2022
🇦🇷 Argentina 3 - 3 France 🇫🇷
(Argentina won 4 - 2 on penalties)

The Greatest Football Match Ever Played

Messi scored a 23rd-minute penalty and a 108th-minute extra-time goal in a thrilling 3-3 epic vs France. Emi Martínez made a legendary 123rd-minute foot save on Kolo Muani, before Argentina converted all 4 penalties (Messi, Dybala, Paredes, Montiel) to win their 3rd FIFA World Cup title!

Messi: 2 Final Goals + Shootout Goal Golden Ball MVP
